This copy of the app could not be read by the browser — usually a file that was damaged in transit or left half-merged. Your writing is not in this file. It is in this browser's own storage, untouched, and a working copy of the app will find it exactly where it was.

What to do:

  1. Reload the page — a stale cached copy is the commonest cause.
  2. If it says this again, get a fresh index.html and open that. Nothing you have written is lost by replacing it.
